No-BS FAQ

Straight answers on privacy, cloud mail, and agents.

Does this make my email "zero cloud"?

No—and we won't lie to you. Your email still lives at Google, Microsoft, or your IMAP host. EveryBox prevents a new third-party AI SaaS from copying your mailbox, and creates a hard local security boundary between your synced mail and the AI agents running on your PC.

Why not just install a free Gmail MCP server?

Because a raw Gmail MCP only covers one account, has no prompt-injection sanitizer, exposes your 2FA codes and private folders, and gives you zero visual interface to review and approve outbound drafts before an agent sends them.

Can an AI agent send emails behind my back?

Not by default. Mutations are off out of the box. When you enable agent drafting, every outbound send or calendar invite routes into EveryBox's visual Approval Queue until you explicitly approve it.

Which AI models does EveryBox support?

Any MCP-compatible agent (Claude, Codex, OpenClaw/Hermes, Cursor) can connect to EveryBox. For in-app summarization, pre-drafting, and vision inspection, connect local Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or your own Anthropic/OpenAI/Gemini/Grok API key.
